Help

This tool compares the bank statement with the company's books for one month, suggests how each record should be treated, and asks people to confirm every suggestion. Nothing is accepted automatically.

How a month is reconciled

  1. Start a new month. Upload the bank statement, the company books for the cash account and last month's open items. The files are checked before anything else happens.
  2. Review. The system sorts every record into a list. Work through each list: confirm the matches, explain the items with no partner, and send anything doubtful to a senior reviewer.
  3. Update the reports. The reports confirm every approved item. An item counts as reconciled only after this check.
  4. Close the month. The Controller reviews the reconciliation statement, explains any remaining difference, and signs off. The month is then locked.

Confidence and risk

Confidence
How sure the system is that a suggested match is right. It decides only the order you see items in, never the outcome. A low-confidence suggestion can still be correct: read the evidence.
Evidence for and against
Every suggestion lists what supports it and what doesn't. Anything that doesn't fit is shown in red, never left out.
Risk
Set by fixed rules, not by the system's guess: large amounts, possible duplicates and unexplained items are high risk and go to a senior reviewer.

Words used on the statement

Bank-to-book reconciliation statement
Starts from the bank's balance and the company's balance and shows every reason they differ. If everything is explained, the bank balance after timing differences equals the book balance after corrections.
Bank balance after timing differences
The bank's balance plus deposits it hasn't received yet, minus checks it hasn't paid yet.
Book balance after corrections
The company's balance plus or minus bank fees, interest and other bank items not yet in the books.
Difference still to explain
What is left unexplained. It must be explained in a comment before the month can close.
Adjustment
A correcting entry the accounting team should make in the books. This tool proposes and approves adjustments; it never posts them.
Audit trail
A permanent, numbered record of every action. Each entry is linked to the one before, so any change to a past entry would be detected.
Reopening
Unlocking a closed month to correct it. It needs a reason and a second person's approval, and the month must be signed off again.
